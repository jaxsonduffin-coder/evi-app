Privacy Policy — EVI

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

EVI ("we", "us", or "our") is operated by JD North LLC, based in Idaho, USA. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the EVI mobile application (the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By using EVI, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password (encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Household information: Details about your home, vehicles, appliances, utilities, tasks, documents, and calendar events that you enter.
  • Uploaded documents: PDFs, images, and other files you choose to upload to your household vault.
  • Communications: Messages you send to our AI assistant ("Ask EVI") or support team.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with the app (features used, time in app, crash reports).
  • Device information: Device type, OS version, unique device identifiers, IP address.
  • Analytics: Aggregated usage patterns via Google Firebase Analytics.

2.3 Information from AI Processing

When you upload documents or use "Ask EVI", we process the content to extract structured data (dates, amounts, etc.) using OpenAI's API. Uploaded content is not used to train external AI models.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Extract and organize information from your documents (AI processing)
  • Generate proactive alerts and reminders based on your data
  • Enable household members to share and collaborate
  • Send you service-related notifications (opt-out available)
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, security, and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share limited data only with:

  • Household members you explicitly add to your household
  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (Google Firebase for hosting/database/storage; OpenAI for AI processing) — bound by contracts requiring data protection
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the United States
  • Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt/scrypt)
  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3
  • Documents are stored in access-controlled cloud storage
  • We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure only authorized household members can access their household's data

6. Data Retention

  • We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • Upon account deletion, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained longer if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., billing records)

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to: Access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and associated data, export your data in a portable format, object to certain types of processing, and withdraw consent.

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@jdnorth.co.

7.1 California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

7.2 European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing is (1) performance of a contract with you, (2) your consent, and (3) our legitimate interests in providing and improving the Service.

8. Children's Privacy

EVI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Services

EVI integrates with: Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage) — Privacy Policy; OpenAI (AI processing) — Privacy Policy; Apple/Google (payment processing via App Store/Play Store).

Each service has its own privacy practices we recommend you review.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
