### Description (4000 chars max)

**Meet EVI — the AI that runs your household so you don't have to.**

Ever forget when your car registration expires? Lost track of that warranty? Wondering when you last serviced your HVAC? EVI is the AI-powered household manager designed to end that chaos.

**EVERYTHING YOUR HOME NEEDS. ONE APP.**

📁 **Smart Document Vault**
Upload leases, warranties, receipts, insurance policies — anything. EVI's AI reads them, extracts key dates and amounts, and organizes them automatically. Search across all your documents instantly.

🏠 **Home Profile**
Whether you rent or own, EVI tracks what matters: lease terms, landlord contacts, purchase details, square footage, systems, and more. All in one place.

🚗 **Vehicle Management**
Track every car in your household — registration dates, insurance, maintenance history, VIN, license plate. Get reminders 30 days before anything expires.

🔧 **Appliance Tracker**
Log every appliance with brand, model, warranty date, and manuals. EVI reminds you before warranties expire so you can extend coverage or replace strategically.

📅 **Household Calendar**
Every deadline in one view. Bills, renewals, maintenance, appointments. Color-coded by urgency.

💬 **Ask EVI Anything**
"When does my lease end?" "What warranties do I have?" "What do I need to do before winter?" Just ask. EVI knows your household inside and out.

🔔 **Proactive Alerts**
EVI doesn't wait for you to check. Urgent, warning, and info alerts surface exactly when they matter. Never miss what's important.

👥 **Share With Your Household**
Add roommates, family members, or partners. Everyone sees the same calendar, tasks, and documents. Work as a team.

**HOW EVI IS DIFFERENT**

Most productivity apps expect you to enter everything. EVI actually reads your documents and does the work for you. Upload once — it extracts dates, amounts, contact info, and creates reminders automatically.
