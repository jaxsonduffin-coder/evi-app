# Family Sharing Setup (iOS Subscriptions)

Apple's Family Sharing lets iCloud family members share a subscription. Enabling it makes the Household plan feel more valuable — one purchase covers the whole family.

## To enable for EVI subscriptions:

1. Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps
2. Select EVI → **Subscriptions** in the sidebar
3. For each subscription product (Solo and Household):
   - Click the product name
   - Scroll to **Family Sharing** section
   - Toggle **"Turn On"**
   - Save

## What happens:

- When a user subscribes on their Apple ID, everyone in their iCloud Family Sharing group (up to 5 people) automatically gets access to EVI Solo/Household features
- The subscription is billed once to the primary payer
- Great for households where multiple people already share iCloud

## Marketing angle:

Once turned on, add to your App Store description:

> **Family Sharing included** — Your iCloud family gets access with a single subscription.

## Note on the Household plan specifically:

The Household plan already supports up to 10 members via your own invite system. Family Sharing is complementary — someone could:
- Subscribe to Household plan ($9.99/mo)
- Share via iCloud Family with 5 people (they auto-get access)
- Also manually invite up to 10 people to their EVI household

This layered approach is a strong retention lever.
