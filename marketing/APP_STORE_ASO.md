## Notes

- Apple's keyword field and Play Store's description are the two places search ranking actually gets influenced — everything else (name, subtitle) matters more for conversion once someone's already looking at the listing.
- Re-check keyword performance in App Store Connect's "Keyword Rankings" (under Analytics) about 2 weeks after launch and adjust the keyword field — it can be changed with every new version submission.
- The subtitle can be A/B tested (Apple's Product Page Optimization / Custom Product Pages) once you have enough install volume to get a signal — worth revisiting after the first few hundred downloads, not on day one.
